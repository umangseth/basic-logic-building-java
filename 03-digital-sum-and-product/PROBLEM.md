# 3. Digital Sum and Product

```
Difficulty: Easy
Es mated Time Limit: 15 minutes

Problem Statement
A number-analysis applica on needs to process the digits of a given posi ve integer.
Your task is to calculate:
   1. The sum of all digits.
   2. The product of all digits.
   3. Whether the number is divisible by 3 based on its digit sum.
The program must process the number digit by digit using a loop.

Input Format
A single posi ve integer N.

Output Format
Print:
Sum: <sum>
Product: <product>
Divisible by 3: Yes/No

Constraints
    1 ≤ N ≤ 10^9

Example
 Input                                                     Output
 234                                                       Sum: 9
                                                           Product: 24
                                                           Divisible by 3: Yes

Explana on
Digits are 2, 3, 4.
Sum:
2+3+4=9
Product:
2 × 3 × 4 = 24
Since 9 is divisible by 3, the number is divisible by 3.
```
